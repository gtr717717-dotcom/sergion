Our Story

We started Sergion because we were tired of tools that got in the way. So we built one that doesn't.

Our Mission

Sergion was founded in 2022 with a single goal: make software teams more effective without adding complexity. We've seen what happens when great ideas get buried under tooling overhead — projects slow down, engineers burn out, and the work that matters most gets delayed.

We believe the best tools disappear into your workflow. They should feel natural, stay out of your way, and give you clarity when you need it most. That's the standard we hold ourselves to with every feature we ship.

Today we work with thousands of teams — from early-stage startups to established engineering organizations — and every day we learn something new about what it means to truly support the work of building software.

What We Believe

Simplicity first

Every feature earns its place. We'd rather ship fewer things well than many things poorly.

Teams over individuals

Software is a team sport. We design for collaboration from day one, not as an afterthought.

Radical transparency

We're honest about what's working, what isn't, and what we're still figuring out.

Bias for action

A good decision today beats a perfect decision next quarter. We ship, learn, and iterate.
